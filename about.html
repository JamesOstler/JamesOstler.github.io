Jim Ostler

Jim Ostler is Associate Dean of Graduate Programs and Associate Professor and JELD-WEN Chair of Free Enterprise at Willamette University's Atkinson School of Business.

His research examines how rules and knowledge structures shape decisions under uncertainty. One stream follows regulation through the institutions and strategic responses that turn rules into functioning markets. A second examines how representations, records, culture, and shared knowledge become organizational capability.

Before joining Willamette, he held faculty appointments at the Wharton School of the University of Pennsylvania, Georgetown University's McDonough School of Business, and the University of Michigan's Ross School of Business. Before academia, he applied advanced analytics to strategic marketing and supply-chain decisions in industry, consulting, and a software startup.

He earned a PhD in Strategic Management and Decision-Making and an MA in Economics from UCLA. He also holds an MBA, an MS in Engineering with a minor in Statistics, and a BS in Chemical Engineering from Brigham Young University.
