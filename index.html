Three habits of thought

Mechanisms.
Incentives.
Interaction.

Formal systems rarely determine outcomes on their own. People must perceive, interpret, coordinate, and act.

Engineering taught me to look for mechanisms. Economics taught me to look for incentives. Strategy taught me to examine the interplay of competitive positions, actions, and responses. Those habits shape both my research and my teaching.
